Frequently Asked

Quick answers to common questions.

Do you offer custom sizes and surface chemistries?

Yes — most of our nanoparticles and magnetic beads can be customized in size, concentration, solvent, and surface functionalization. Contact us with your specification.

What is your typical lead time?

Catalog products ship within 3–5 business days. Custom synthesis and assay development timelines depend on scope — we provide a milestone plan at project kickoff.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we ship worldwide with appropriate documentation for biological and nanomaterial shipments. Contact sales for a quote including freight and duties.

Can I request a sample before ordering?

Yes — evaluation samples are available for most product lines. Reach out with your application details and we'll arrange it.
