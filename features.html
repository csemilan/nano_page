Product Offerings

We specialize in the manufacture and supply of advanced nanomaterials for diagnostics, biosensors, and life-science research.

Gold Nanoparticles and Functionalities

High-quality spherical and anisotropic gold nanoparticles with customizable surface chemistries — carboxyl, amine, maleimide, streptavidin, protein A/G, and antibody conjugates. Available in 5–200 nm diameters with tight CV for reproducible assay performance.

  • 5 – 200 nm sizes
  • Custom optical density & volume
  • Conjugation-ready surfaces

Silica Nanoparticles and Functionalities

Precision-engineered silica nanoparticles with uniform size distribution, low aggregation, and versatile surface chemistry. Ideal for lateral-flow conjugates, fluorescence labels, and biosensor substrates.

  • 30 – 500 nm diameters
  • Dye-doped and plain options
  • Amine, thiol, carboxyl surfaces

Magnetic Particles and Functionalities

Superparamagnetic beads for bioseparation, nucleic-acid extraction, immunoprecipitation, and automated diagnostic workflows. Silica, carboxyl, streptavidin, and protein-coated formats available.

  • 0.5 – 5 µm bead sizes
  • High magnetic response
  • Low non-specific binding

Fusion Particles and Functionalities

Multi-modal fusion particles that integrate optical, magnetic, and catalytic properties into a single platform — enabling multiplexed detection and simplified workflows in modern point-of-care diagnostics.

  • Multimodal detection
  • Custom core-shell designs
  • Scalable synthesis

Service Offerings

Affinity Nano Reagents

Specialized affinity reagents designed to enhance molecular recognition, specificity, and diagnostic accuracy in life-science applications.

Immunoassay Development

Complete ELISA, CLIA, and nano-immunoassay development — from antibody pairing and buffer optimization to full analytical validation.
