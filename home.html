Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

What product sizes and surface chemistries do you offer?

Gold nanoparticles from 5 – 200 nm; silica from 30 – 500 nm; magnetic beads from 0.5 – 5 µm. Surface options include citrate, carboxyl, amine, maleimide, streptavidin, protein A/G, and custom conjugates.

Are your products for research use only?

Yes — unless explicitly stated otherwise, all NanoDx products are sold for research and further manufacturing use only. Customers are responsible for regulatory compliance in their own jurisdiction.

What is your typical lead time?

Catalog items ship within 3 – 5 business days. Custom synthesis and assay development timelines depend on scope — we provide a milestone plan at project kickoff.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we ship worldwide with appropriate documentation for nanomaterial and biological shipments. Contact sales for a quote including freight and duties.

Can I request a sample before ordering?

Yes — evaluation samples are available for most product lines. Reach out with your application details and we'll arrange it.

Do you provide certificates of analysis (CoA)?

Every batch ships with a CoA covering size, concentration, zeta potential, and (where applicable) conjugation efficiency. Lot-specific datasheets are available on request.
