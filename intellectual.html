NanoDx Corp respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects its users, partners, and customers to do the same. This policy describes how we protect our own IP and how you can report potential infringement.

1. Overview

NanoDx Corp owns or licenses a broad portfolio of intellectual property covering our brand, website content, product designs, manufacturing methods, and scientific know-how. All rights not expressly granted in writing are reserved.

2. Trademarks

The following are trademarks or registered trademarks of NanoDx Corp in the United States and other jurisdictions:

  • Product-line names and taglines used on packaging and marketing materials

You may not use our trademarks without prior written permission, except for accurate, descriptive references such as "compatible with NanoDx products".

3. Copyrights

All content on this website — including text, graphics, images, product descriptions, datasheets, protocols, illustrations, and layout — is protected by copyright. © 2021–2026 NanoDx Corp. All rights reserved.

You may not reproduce, distribute, modify, create derivative works from, publicly display, or publicly perform any content without our prior written consent, except as permitted under fair use.

4. Patents

Certain NanoDx products, formulations, and methods are covered by one or more pending or issued patents. The purchase of a product does not convey any license, express or implied, under any patent rights owned or controlled by NanoDx, except the limited right to use the product for its intended purpose.

5. Trade Secrets & Know-How

Our manufacturing processes, reagent formulations, surface chemistries, and assay protocols contain valuable trade secrets and proprietary know-how. These are protected by confidentiality agreements with employees, contractors, and partners.

Unauthorized use or disclosure of our trade secrets may result in both civil and criminal liability under applicable laws, including the U.S. Defend Trade Secrets Act.

6. Permitted Use

Without further permission, you may:

  • View and browse this website for personal or internal business purposes
  • Download a single copy of datasheets for your internal reference
  • Share links to our website or product pages
  • Quote short excerpts with clear attribution to NanoDx Corp

For any other use — including reproduction, republishing, translation, or commercial distribution — you must obtain prior written permission from legal@nanodxcorp.com.

7. Product IP Rights

Products sold by NanoDx are protected by a combination of patents, trade secrets, and know-how. The following restrictions apply to all NanoDx products unless otherwise stated in writing:

  • Products may not be reverse-engineered, disassembled, or chemically analyzed to determine their composition or method of manufacture
  • Products may not be repackaged, relabeled, or sold as another manufacturer's product
  • Products may not be used to develop competing products
  • Products may not be exported in violation of U.S. export control laws

8. Customer Intellectual Property

When you engage NanoDx for custom development services — including custom antibody development, assay design, or surface functionalization — the following principles apply:

  • You retain ownership of any materials, targets, or specifications you provide to us
  • Unless otherwise agreed, NanoDx retains ownership of its pre-existing background IP and any improvements to it
  • Ownership of newly developed foreground IP will be set out in a separate written agreement
  • We will not use your confidential materials for any purpose other than delivering the requested services

9. Reporting Infringement

If you believe that content on our website, or a product we sell, infringes your intellectual property rights, please notify us in writing. Your notice should include:

  1. Your name, address, email, and phone number
  2. A description of the IP right you believe is being infringed (registration number, if applicable)
  3. The specific URL or product where the alleged infringement occurs
  4. A statement of your good-faith belief that the use is unauthorized
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorized to act on their behalf
  6. Your physical or electronic signature

Send notices to legal@nanodxcorp.com.
